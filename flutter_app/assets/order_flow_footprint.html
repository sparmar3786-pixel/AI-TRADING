📖 Order Flow – User Manual

1. Ye chart kya hai?

Normal candle sirf Open-High-Low-Close dikhati hai. Order flow (footprint) chart har candle ke andar dikhata hai ki kis price pe kitna buying aur kitna selling hua.

2. Cell kaise padhe

Har price level pe Sell × Buy likha hota hai. Example 120 × 340 = us price pe 120 qty sellers ne bechi, 340 qty buyers ne khareedi. 1.2k = 1,200, 2.5L = 2.5 lakh.

3. Colors

  • Green cell: buyers zyada.
  • Red cell: sellers zyada.
  • Jitna gehra color, utna zyada volume.
  • Left ki patli candle: green = upar band, red = neeche band.

4. Peela box = POC

Candle ka sabse zyada volume wala price. Yahan sabse zyada ladai hui, isliye ye support/resistance ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai.

5. Bold number = Imbalance

Jab ek side doosri side se 3x ya zyada ho (buy ko ek level neeche ke sell se, sell ko ek level upar ke buy se compare karte hain). Bold hara = aggressive buying, bold laal = aggressive selling. Lagatar kai levels pe imbalance = strong pressure.

6. Candle ke neeche

  • Δ (Delta) = Buy − Sell volume. Plus (hara) = buyers jeete, minus (laal) = sellers jeete.
  • Vol = total volume.
  • Buy ord / Sell ord = bid aur ask ke best 5 levels me kitne orders lage hain.

7. Padhne ke simple tareeke

  • Price upar + Δ plus = move mazboot.
  • Price upar par Δ minus = kamzor move (divergence), palatne ka chance.
  • Neeche ke levels pe bhaari sell volume ke baad bhi price na gire = buyers absorb kar rahe hain.

Ye sirf samajhne ke liye hai, trading advice nahi.

8. Status (upar right)

  • ● LIVE = market khula, live data aa raha hai.
  • AFTER MARKET = market band, last save hua data dikh raha hai (date + time ke saath).
  • Reconnecting = net/server issue, apne aap dobara judega.

9. Controls

  • Dropdown: index aur timeframe (1/3/5/15/30 min, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week) badlo.
  • Chart ko ungli/mouse se drag karo = peeche ki candles.
  • + / − ya mouse wheel = zoom.
  • Live ⇥ = latest candle pe wapas.
  • Phone ko landscape karo = zyada candles dikhengi.

10. Zaroori baatein

  • Buyer/seller estimate hai (LTP ko bid/ask se compare karke), exchange ka exact aggressor data nahi.
  • Index ka data uske nearest-expiry futures se aata hai, kyunki index spot pe volume nahi hota.
  • Order count sirf top-5 depth ka hai.
  • Data server chalu hone ke baad se banta hai.
